Why this exists

I spent months looking for one good source. I never found it.

Search “credit cards explained” and you find creators recommending which card to sign up for, blogs full of affiliate links, and bank pages written like legal documents.

What I wanted was simple — just tell me how the thing actually works. Who gets paid. What each fee is. What “utilisation” really means. Why minimum payments are a trap.

So I figured it out, wrote it down, and turned it into this.

“If you understand the mechanics, you stop being the one who pays for everyone else’s rewards.”
i.

Do, then read

Every chapter opens with something to tap, drag or decide. The explanation comes after you have seen the result.

ii.

Your country’s rules

A late fee in Mumbai, Miami, Manchester and Melbourne are four different things. Pick an edition and every number, law and example changes.
