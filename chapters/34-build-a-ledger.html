Build a Ledger

Under every bank, card and payment app sits a ledger. You keep this one. For each event, choose which account is debited and which is credited. If you get it right, the books balance. Then we break them.

The rules, in one line
Debit and creditEvery movement is recorded twice. A debit increases an asset (what the bank owns) or reduces a liability (what it owes). A credit does the opposite. Your deposit is the bank’s liability: it owes you that money. Income, such as fees, is a credit.
