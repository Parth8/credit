Before anything goes wrong

Five habits that win disputes.

Pay strangers by card

Never by bank transfer, UPI, gift card or crypto. The way you pay is the protection you get.

Screenshot the deal

The listing, the price, the delivery promise, the terms. Pages change after a dispute starts.

Keep it in writing

Email or chat, not phone calls. Dates and words are evidence; memories are not.

Act on the alert

Turn on instant transaction alerts. Many protections depend on reporting within days.

Name the right thing

Say “unauthorised”, “not received”, “not as described”, “cancelled”. Using the right category gets the right rule.
