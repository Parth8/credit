Two perspectives

Why does the minimum exist?

The bank’s design

Keep you just able to pay

  • The minimum is set low enough that almost anyone can meet it, and high enough that the account never defaults
  • In the early years most of each minimum payment is interest, not debt
  • Interest on carried balances is the largest source of card revenue
  • As the balance falls, so does the minimum, stretching the debt out for years
Your move

Pay it in full. Always.

  • Autopay the full statement balance: take the decision away from tired, future you
  • Treat the card as a short float, never as a loan
  • If you cannot clear it, pay a fixed amount and never let it fall with the minimum
  • Stop using the card while you are paying it down: new spending restarts the clock
  • If it would not fit in this month’s budget in cash, it does not go on the card
