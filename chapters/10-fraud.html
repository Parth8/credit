Six scams that are running in {name} right now. Each one looks ordinary. Decide what you would do before you see the answer, that is how the instinct gets built.

The pattern behind all six

Every scam needs three things.

1.

A borrowed identity

Your bank, the police, a courier, your child. Sender names, caller ID and voices can all be faked.

2.

A clock

“Within 24 hours.” “Before the account is frozen.” Urgency exists to stop you checking.

3.

You doing the work

Tapping the link, reading out the code, moving the money. Modern fraud rarely breaks in: it asks you to open the door.

One rule beats all of themStop. End the conversation. Contact the organisation yourself, using a number or address you already trust: the back of your card, the official app. A real problem will still be there in ten minutes. A scam will not.
