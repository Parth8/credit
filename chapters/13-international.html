RULE OF THUMB

Always pay in the local currency. Banks convert at near-market rates with a small markup. DCC ("pay in INR") applies a 5-7% terrible rate locked at the merchant — that\'s their business model. The "rate locked, no surprises" framing exists precisely to make this look like the safe choice. It\'s the opposite.
