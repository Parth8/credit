Before you file

The evidence that wins.

Proof of the deal

Order confirmation, invoice, the listing or advert as it looked when you bought: screenshot it, because pages change.

Proof you tried

Dated emails or chat logs asking the merchant to fix it. Banks nearly always ask whether you tried the merchant first.

Proof of the problem

Photos, video, tracking that never moved, cancellation confirmations. Keep it factual and in date order.

Do not abuse itDisputing a purchase you made and received is “friendly fraud”. Merchants contest it with delivery and login records, you lose, and issuers do close accounts over it.
