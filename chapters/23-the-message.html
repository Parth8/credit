Anatomy of an Authorisation

That 1.4-second beep is a message of about thirty numbered fields crossing six computers and back. Watch it travel, open it up, then sit in the issuer’s chair and answer six of them yourself.

Scene 2 · Open the envelope

The message is just numbered fields.

Almost every card payment on earth speaks ISO 8583, a format from 1987: a message type, then “data elements” by number. Tap each field of your coffee purchase.
