Where this is heading

The card is losing its parts.

Numberless cards

Newer cards print no number, expiry or code at all: the details live in the app, where they can be hidden, frozen or regenerated.

The stripe retires

Mastercard stopped requiring the magnetic stripe on new cards in Europe from 2024, the US follows from 2027, and it disappears from all new cards by 2033.

The phone is the card

Apple Pay and Google Pay never transmit your real number: they send a one-off token. The plastic becomes the backup, not the main event.
