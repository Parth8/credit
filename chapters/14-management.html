Managing Like a Pro

Six months of ordinary life with a card. Each month something happens and you choose what to do. Your score and your money keep a running total.

The routine behind good decisions

Ten minutes a month. That is all.

Autopay in full

Set once. It removes the single most expensive mistake from your life.

Read the statement

Five minutes: unknown charges, creeping subscriptions, fees. Dispute anything odd the same day.

Watch the statement-day balance

That is the figure the bureaus see. Before a big application, pay it down early.

Review once a year

Is the fee still earning its keep? Ask for a waiver, a downgrade, or close the card, keeping your oldest one open.

Six months, handled.

None of it was complicated. It was just a string of small decisions, each with a price. Next: the benefits you are already paying for.
